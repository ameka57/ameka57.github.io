About the project

This research explores how perfectionism and procrastination can influence the way students approach their academic tasks. The project also examines the role of self-control and individual habits.

What are we studying?

Perfectionism

The tendency to set very high standards for yourself and to be strongly concerned about mistakes.

Procrastination

The tendency to delay tasks even when you know that completing them may be important.

Self-control

The ability to regulate your actions, attention and behaviour in order to achieve a goal.
